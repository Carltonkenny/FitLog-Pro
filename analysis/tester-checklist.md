# FitLog Pro: tester checklist

Thanks for testing! This is an early version, so please be honest. Nothing here is a test of you. If something is confusing, that is our fault.

Link: <paste the app link here>
Time needed: about 15 minutes now, then use it for your real workouts for two weeks.

## First time (do these in order)
1. Open the link on your phone. Tap **Start free**, create an account. Did anything confuse you?
2. Answer the 4 questions. Could you pick answers that match you?
3. On the workout screen, tap the green check next to a set. Change a weight with the + and - buttons. Add one set.
4. Tap **Add exercise**. Look at "Suggested for you". Do the suggestions make sense? Tap the star on one you like.
5. Tap **Finish** after completing at least one set. Does the summary look right?
6. Tap **Repeat last workout**. Then **Save as routine** and name it.
7. Tap the little face button (your companion). Tell it something about you, for example "I only have dumbbells and I love pull-ups". Then ask "What should I train today?" or "Add a goblet squat to my workout".
8. In the companion, tap **Memory**. Can you see and delete what it remembers?
9. Open **Settings**. Switch kg and lb. Change the rest timer.
10. Install it on your home screen (Settings shows how).
11. Put your phone in airplane mode. Log a set. Turn airplane mode off. Check that your workout is still there.
12. Optional: attach a photo of your gym equipment or a lift to the companion and ask for a plan.

## During the two weeks
Use it for your real workouts. Note anything that slowed you down at the gym: buttons too small, too many taps, confusing words.

## If something goes wrong
Send me:
- What you were trying to do
- What you expected
- What actually happened (a screenshot helps a lot)
- Your phone and browser (for example "iPhone 13, Safari")

## Questions for you at the end
1. What did you mainly use it for?
2. What was confusing or annoying?
3. What did you wish it could do?
4. Would you use it every week? Why or why not?
5. Did the companion feel useful, or gimmicky?

Privacy: your workouts are saved to your own account. If you send a photo to the companion it is resized, stripped of location data and sent to Google Gemini to answer you. It is not stored by the app. You can delete anything the companion remembers in Memory.
