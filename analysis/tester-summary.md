## What it is
A fast, simple way to log your gym workouts, see your progress, and get advice from an AI coach that actually knows your training.

## What it does
- **Log sets in two taps.** Big buttons, your last weights are already filled in, and a rest timer starts when you tick a set.
- **Works without internet.** Log at the gym with no signal. It saves on your phone and syncs later.
- **Your data follows you.** Sign in on another device and your workouts, routines and plan are there. If you log on your phone, your laptop updates by itself.
- **Routines.** "Repeat last workout" and save your own routines (Push, Pull, Legs...).
- **Smart exercise picks.** Over 100 exercises. It suggests what to train based on your goal, your equipment, and which muscles you have neglected this week, and tells you why.
- **Your coach (the little face button).** Tell it things ("I only have dumbbells", "my knee hurts on lunges") and it remembers. Ask it what to train, ask for a weekly plan (you can add a photo of your gym or your form), or tell it to add an exercise to today's workout. You can see and delete everything it remembers under **Memory**.
- **Progress.** Your estimated max lifts, sets per muscle this week, your streak, and your personal records.
- **Install it like an app** on your home screen.

## What I would love you to try (15 minutes now, then real use for 2 weeks)
1. Sign up and answer the 4 questions.
2. Log a few sets, finish the workout, tap **Repeat last workout**, then **Save as routine**.
3. Open **Add exercise** and look at "Suggested for you". Does it make sense?
4. Tap the face button. Tell it about your gear. Ask "What should I train today?" and "Build my plan".
5. Turn on airplane mode, log a set, turn it off again. Is your workout still there?
6. Add it to your home screen.
7. Use it for your real workouts. Note anything that slowed you down at the gym.

## Privacy, in plain words
Your workouts are saved to your own account and only you can see them. If you send the coach a photo, it is shrunk, stripped of location data and sent to Google's Gemini to answer you. I do not store it. The coach's memory is yours: you can view and delete it any time. The coach gives general training tips, not medical advice.
